*Disclaimer: This event happened quite a long time ago, but despite that, I really wanted to write down my experience. I wrote a version that was quite in-depth, ready to be published, but apparently I didn't commit it and switched distros. Alas. This blogpost might be therefore a bit shorter than you might have expected. I recommend [Nick's blog](https://blog.nickesselman.nl/beest) as well!*

I was delighted to hear the news that there was going to be a Hack Club event in The Netherlands. Most of them were either too far away for me, or the timing was simply inconvenient. Thus, I did what anyone would do in my situation and signed up.

On arriving in the Stayokay hostel in The Hague, I met a lot of my online friends in real life for the first time. This was a great experience, and I'm really grateful to Hack Club for organising these kinds of events. I went out to the city for a bit to chat with Peter and Mathias and we got some chips. After that, we chilled at the hostel for a bit.

The first day, we went to the venue to build a strandbeest ourselves. Or at least try to. I helped a bit with assembling the legs and core, chatted with some other people including Evan, and went out in the afternoon to get lunch.

![The Strandbeest being assembled](https://photos.hackclub.com/media/568b448d-92a5-4e94-9c05-beb458ca0d90?sig=e0T_oVW9y7gm4JazalXePIkgRllNn5vGDKuNWiMhwCY&size=lg&fmt=avif)

![The Hague city](/blog-assets/the-hague.jpg)

On day 2, we finished the Strandbeest. It looked quite nice in the end, to be honest! It sadly didn't walk very well, but that didn't matter. It was just a lot of fun assembling it.

![The finished strandbeest](https://photos.hackclub.com/events/beest?photo=0fcabf2c-c9b2-456d-8252-d9090fd25664)

We also went to the strandbeest morturarium. Two and a half decades of unused material was displayed there, it was very impressive to see!

![The morturarium](https://photos.hackclub.com/media/68060b25-f313-4a5e-b302-d1dca789f07c?sig=8zTkbuggGJfYwSP163b0CHMeUuvJXzh3CuTjj6NE8wc&size=lg&fmt=avif)

We decided to go to the cinema together that day. We watched The Oddyssey, and it was my first time seeing a movie in IMAX (even though it was 'fake IMAX', according to Juan ;) The only problem was - the movie ended at 1am and it started before dinner was catered at the hostel. But luckily, McDonald's was open.

And don't ask me why, but afterwards we decided to do a 'mini-hackathon' in our dorms where we made a website in 10 minutes. [Mine](https://swiftdust.github.io/beest-3am-website/) is honestly pretty terrible, but I was also trying to learn Vim alongside it - it was about my third time using the software - which might not have been the smartest idea?

Day three was already the final day! We went to see the Strandbeests at the beach in The Hague and got to meet Theo Jansen in real life. This was genuinely so cool, especially because it is really unique - you can't  buy tickets, but need to be invited by Theo Jansen himself! I loved the hydraulic pressure system he showed off the most:

Then, it was already time for the closing ceremony. We got a very nice Beest shirt, I wear it to school quite often! I could've went home, but it was way too *gezellig* (fun) to go yet! Therefore I decided to stay one more day. We had some very nice Curaçaoan food (Curaçao is a constituent country in the Kingdom of The Netherlands, in the Carribean!) and then rented some bikes and toured around the city one more time.

The next day we unfortunately had to say goodbye. I made the best friends, had some great experiences and continued about the little that was left of my summer holiday, my life changed.
